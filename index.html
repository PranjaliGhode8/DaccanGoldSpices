From Indian Roots to Global Kitchens

A heritage built on purity, sustainability, and direct farm relations.

With a single motto to deliver premium & fine quality spices to Global buyers, Deccan Gold Spices was founded in Nagpur in 2026.

While globally celebrated as the "Orange City" and the "Tiger Capital of India," Nagpur holds a third, deeply potent identity that remains one of the culinary world’s best-kept secrets: its extraordinary, fiery spice heritage!

We bring to you all the magic of the Deccan plateau in the form of it's purest of spices !
