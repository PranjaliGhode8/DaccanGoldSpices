From Indian Roots to Global Kitchens

A heritage built on purity, sustainability, and direct farm relations.

"Spices are not just flavorings; they are the warm memory of Indian sunshine and the heartbeat of home-cooked meals."

A Third Generation Promise

Deccan Gold Spice began as a small apothecary chest in Pune, Maharashtra. Over seventy years ago, our grandfather hand-selected cardamom pods and turmeric fingers for families seeking authentic herbal remedies. Today, we bring those exact same principles of hand-sorting and chemical-free sourcing to home cooks and world-class chefs worldwide.

Direct Sourcing & Biodiverse Farming

Unlike commercial spice companies that purchase raw ingredients from mass bulk warehouses, we select our partner farms individually. Our green cardamom comes from a family estate in Munnar, Kerala that uses natural canopy shade and organic compost. Our turmeric comes from Sangli, harvested at peak curcumin maturity. By working directly with farming collectives, we double standard auction rates, guaranteeing financial freedom for rural workers and preserving centuries of agricultural knowledge.

Cold Milled & Hand Packed

Commercial spice processing uses high-speed steel mills that generate heat up to 80°C. This heat vaporizes the delicate essential oils that contain the aroma, flavor, and therapeutic properties of spices. At Deccan Gold, we use slow-speed cold-granulation stone milling. We pack our spices in UV-filtering amber glass jars right at the source, preserving absolute freshness and warm, homely flavor from our home to yours.
